Options can expire worthless. Time decay, implied volatility, liquidity, exercise, assignment and broker margin requirements can materially affect results.

You decide independently whether to trade, through which broker, at what time, in what size and with what risk controls. A reference entry, target or stop-loss level is not an executable quote, a broker order or a guarantee that a price will be reached. Gaps, halts, spreads, slippage, fees, financing and taxes can produce a materially different outcome. A stop-loss reference does not guarantee a maximum loss.

4. Targets, probabilities and reported performance

Past performance is not a guarantee of future results. Targets and potential gains are assumptions or scenarios, not promised returns. A displayed probability is an estimate and should not be treated as an independently validated likelihood of success unless a supporting methodology is expressly provided.

Unless expressly stated with a supporting methodology, signal gains or averages are not an audited portfolio, your realised brokerage returns, or a complete record of every recommendation. Selected results can omit losses, open positions, timing differences, costs, taxes and other adjustments. Do not infer a reliable income, win rate or achievable return from an example.

Website images can be edited interface previews showing sample transactions or hypothetical setups. A real ticker or historical price does not establish that the app issued or executed the displayed trade. For a simple long-stock illustration, potential gain = (target − entry) ÷ entry × 100. Correct arithmetic does not make a hypothetical target a forecast or evidence that it can be achieved. Read each preview’s caption for its scope and reference date.

6. Unusual options activity and flow bias

Live Flow screens options-contract activity using available data. Estimated flow value, volume/open-interest ratios, bullish/bearish summaries and labels such as whale, burst, spike or fresh are research indicators. They do not identify the beneficial trader or prove that a trade opened a new position, represents informed activity, is directional or will be profitable.

Calls and puts can be parts of hedges, closing trades or multi-leg strategies. An estimated flow premium is not necessarily one order’s actual cash value or an investor’s profit. Filter changes can change the bias meter because they change the visible records being summarised. Do not interpret “smart money” as guaranteed institutional insight or information unavailable to the public.

7. Political and corporate-insider disclosures

Political Flow and Insider Flow show reported disclosures, not live surveillance of a person’s trades. The transaction date can precede its filing or disclosure date. Reported amounts can be ranges, and ownership can relate to a spouse, dependent, trust or another reported holder. Corrections and explanatory filing notes can change how a record should be understood.

A purchase or sale is different from an award, exercise, gift, conversion or shares withheld for tax. A filing can include multiple transactions. Review the underlying filing when available; do not infer a person’s motive, wrongdoing, possession of inside information, endorsement of the app or the suitability of a similar trade for you.

The Service is not an execution-critical monitoring system. We do not guarantee that an alert arrives before other recipients see the information or before a price changes. Confirm the underlying record and relevant dates inside the app.
